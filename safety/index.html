TRUST & SAFETY

Safety built in.
Not as an afterthought.

We built NEYO so you can meet people around you with peace of mind. Here is how your identity, chats, and location are protected.

Connections First

No stranger can slide into your direct messages or initiate voice calls. Messaging and calls are unlocked only after both people say yes to a connection request.

✓ Zero unsolicited cold messages · Unfriend or block with one tap at any time.

Private Account Mode

Toggle your profile to private at any moment. When private, your photos, moments, memories, and feeds are completely invisible to anyone who isn't already your accepted friend.

✓ Total visibility control · Strangers see only your chosen display name.

Safe & Moderated Communities

Local groups on NEYO are protected by creator-first tools:

  • Public Communities: Appear nearby, but every new member must be individually approved by the creator.
  • Private Communities: Completely invisible from search; accessible only through a friend's private invite code.
  • Creator Power: Group creators are notified of every member and can ban trolls or reset invite codes immediately.

Precise Location is Never Broadcast

Your exact GPS coordinates are never shown on posts. Location tagging on moments is turned off by default. If you choose to tag a place, only the generic place name (like "Pykara Lake") is displayed.

✓ Live location inside communities is strictly opt-in and restricted to verified members during active meetups.

Instant Block & Auto-Hide on 3 Reports

You can block or report any profile, message, post, or group in one tap. When any piece of content receives 3 community reports, it is automatically hidden across all NEYO feeds pending human safety review.

✓ Quick bad-actor suppression · Automatic shielding from toxic behavior.

Firebase App Check & Encryption

All server communications are protected by Firebase App Check and attestation tokens. Unauthorized scripts, scrapers, and malicious bots are blocked before they can ever access backend data.

✓ Encrypted in transit · Strict security rules protecting user collections.

Need to report a safety concern?

Our trust and safety team reviews reports promptly. If you ever experience harassment, impersonation, or feel unsafe, write directly to our priority desk.

Contact Safety Desk (safety@neyo.app)
