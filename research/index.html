OUR CORE BELIEF

Why Local Connection
Changes Everything.

How stepping away from infinite feeds and looking around your own neighborhood can solve modern city loneliness.

The Short Version: We live in some of the most vibrant, bustling cities in India, yet most of us don't know the person living two streets over. NEYO was built to fix that by connecting you to the real world right outside your door.

1. The Strange Paradox of the Modern Indian City

Whether you live in Chennai, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, or Madurai, our cities are full of life. Thousands of people share our morning commutes, work in the same tech parks, or sit at the same tea stalls. Yet studies show that over 80% of young urban adults feel disconnected from the people physically around them.

Traditional social media promised to bring us closer, but it mostly connects us to celebrities, algorithmically generated drama, and influencers thousands of miles away. It leaves the 500 meters around your home completely silent.

2. Your Life in Circles (The 4 Distance Spheres)

In sociology, human comfort is naturally shaped by physical distance. We designed NEYO around four natural circles of life:

3. The Power of "The Third Place"

Sociologists often talk about the "Third Place" — the place you go that isn't home (your first place) and isn't your office or college (your second place). In Indian life, that has always been the corner tea shop, the beach promenade at sunrise, or a quiet park bench.

NEYO brings this concept into the digital age. Instead of joining abstract global forums, you join real communities tied to physical places — your local college club, your street's runners group, or a weekend road trip circle.

4. How We Keep You Safe

Connecting locally only works if you feel 100% safe. That is why NEYO was built from day one with three strict rules:

  • Connections First: Strangers cannot send you direct messages or call you out of the blue. You only chat when you both agree to connect.
  • Never Your Home Address: NEYO never broadcasts your exact GPS coordinates. The app only groups you into broad neighborhood blocks (about 1.2 square km), making it impossible for anyone to pin down your home address.
  • Creator Protection: Every public community is managed by real group owners who vet member requests, keep discussions respectful, and ban disruptive behavior.

5. Memories Belong to Places

Photos posted on standard social media disappear down an endless chronological feed after a few days. But memories in real life are tied to where they happened — that viewpoint at Ooty Lake, that cafe in Indiranagar, that beach sunrise in Chennai.

On NEYO, your shared trips and moments live on the map. When you visit a place with friends, everything you capture compiles into a shared album. When you return a year later, NEYO brings that afternoon right back to you.
