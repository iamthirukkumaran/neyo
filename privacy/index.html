Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

Summary for Quick Reading: NEYO is a hyperlocal social application created to discover people, places, and communities around you. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not serve behavioral advertisement trackers. Location tags on your posts are strictly off by default.

1. Information We Collect

To provide hyperlocal discovery, messaging, and community interactions, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: Your display name, email address, profile photograph, biographical snippet, and self-selected interest tags (e.g. music, trekking, sports).
  • Location Data:
    • Approximate Location: Coarse geolocation (neighborhood or city level) collected while using the app to calculate relative distance to nearby people, places, and communities.
    • Precise Location: High-accuracy GPS coordinates are collected only if you explicitly enable the optional "Live Member Map" feature during an active community meetup.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos, videos (up to 60 seconds with custom cover), 24h stories, comments, community polls, event listings, and buy-and-sell posts.
  • Communications: Direct and group messages, reactions, replies, voice call signaling data, and sticker selections.
  • Social Connections: List of connections you accept, communities you join or manage, and trip memory albums.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging), crash logs, and non-identifying aggregate performance metrics.

2. Why We Collect This Data & How We Use It

We use your information strictly for core app functionality:

  • To calculate distance and discover people, places, and communities within your selected radius (1 km to 35 km).
  • To operate real-time chats, voice calls, and community forums.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding connection requests, community approvals, and direct messages.
  • To maintain trust and safety by identifying spam, harassment, and policy violations.
  • To prevent automated abuse through security verification (Firebase App Check).

3. Where Data is Stored & Processed

We utilize trusted, enterprise-grade cloud service providers:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Authentication (Firebase Auth), database (Cloud Firestore), storage, and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Cloudinary: Optimized, secure encoding and delivery of user video uploads.
  • Open Map APIs & Places: Wikipedia and Google Places APIs for geographical points of interest and landmarks.

4. How Your Location is Protected

Your physical safety and privacy are fundamental to our architecture:

  • Your exact GPS coordinates are never shown to other users on public posts.
  • Location tagging on moments and posts is turned off by default.
  • When you choose to tag a place on a post, only the name of the place is shown (e.g., "Pykara Falls"), not your home or exact location.
  • Live member location within communities is strictly opt-in, ephemeral, and visible only to approved members of that specific community.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or commercial third parties. Data is only shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit direction (e.g. sharing a post to your connections or a community).
  • With our contracted cloud service providers under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by enforceable legal process under Indian jurisdiction or to prevent imminent physical harm.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You own your data. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time directly inside the app:

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Upon confirming deletion, your profile, authentication records, photos, posts, connections, and owned communities are permanently purged from our active databases. For detailed instructions or web-based deletion requests, please visit our Account Deletion Portal.

7. Children's Privacy

NEYO is designed for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact our safety desk immediately at safety@neyo.app for immediate investigation and deletion.

8. Governing Law & Grievance Contact

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
