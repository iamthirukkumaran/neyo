Delete Your Account & Data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Here is exactly how it works.

At NEYO, you own your personal information. When you decide to delete your account, your data is permanently removed from our active production databases.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

The quickest way to delete your account is right inside the NEYO app on your smartphone:

1

Open Profile

Open the NEYO app and tap your Profile tab in the bottom right corner of the navigation bar.

2

Go to Settings

Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner of your profile.

3

Select Account → Delete Account

Scroll to the bottom of the Account section and tap "Delete Account".

4

Confirm Permanent Deletion

Review the summary of data being removed and tap "Permanently Delete My Account". Your session ends and your data is purged immediately.

What Gets Deleted?

  • Profile & Login: Your display name, email/phone credentials, bio, and profile photos are deleted immediately.
  • Moments & Media: All photos, videos, 24h stories, comments, and reactions you posted are removed.
  • Connections: Your connections list is cleared, and pending invitations are cancelled.
  • Communities: Any community where you are the sole creator is disbanded; if co-creators exist, ownership transfers automatically.
  • Messages: Direct 1-on-1 chat threads are deleted. In group chats, your past messages show "[Deleted Account]".

Retention Timeline

Account deletion is immediate in our primary database. Encrypted offline backup snapshots are systematically overwritten in routine cycles within 30 days. No record of your identity or personal contact information is kept.

Method 2: Email Request (If You Already Deleted the App)

If you lost your phone or uninstalled NEYO without deleting your account first, you can request account deletion by emailing our privacy desk from your registered email address:

Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]
Email: delete-account@neyo.app

To ensure security, our team will send a verification confirmation link to your registered email before finalizing the deletion.
