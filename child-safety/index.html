Child Protection Policy

Child Safety Standards

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child exploitation.

Zero-Tolerance Statement: NEYO maintains an uncompromising, zero-tolerance policy regarding any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), predatory behavior, or grooming on our platform.

1. Age Restrictions & Youth Safety

NEYO is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not design features for children, nor do we permit accounts registered by minors without verified adult authorization where permitted by local law. Any account identified as belonging to an underage user is subject to immediate restriction and termination.

2. Automated Detection & Proactive Moderation

To prevent the distribution of illicit material, NEYO employs proactive defenses across our media ingestion pipelines:

  • Media Hashing & Screening: Uploaded visual media is passed through cryptographic perceptual hashing systems to identify and intercept known abuse material before publication.
  • Automated Inappropriate Content Filters: Algorithmic text and media filtering prevents the transmission of sexually suggestive or abusive communications.
  • Three-Strike Community Suppression: Any content flagged by 3 community members is immediately sequestered from all public and community feeds pending safety verification.

3. Mandatory Law Enforcement Reporting

In compliance with global standards, the Indian Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, and international child welfare protocols:

  • Any confirmed detection of CSAM or child exploitation triggers an immediate report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Incidents within India are reported directly to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and relevant state law enforcement authorities.
  • Offending accounts are permanently banned, associated devices are blacklisted, and digital evidence logs are preserved securely for subpoena and prosecution.

4. How to Report a Child Safety Violation

If you witness or suspect any child safety violation, predatory conduct, or inappropriate interaction on NEYO, please take action immediately:

Immediate Reporting Channels:

  • In-App Report: Tap the three dots (•••) on any post, profile, or message and select "Report → Child Safety / Harm".
  • Priority Safety Email: Contact our designated 24/7 child safety response team directly at child-safety@neyo.app.
  • External Emergency Helplines:
    • India Childline: 1098 (Toll-Free, 24/7)
    • National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in (Helpline: 1930)
    • NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org

5. Dedicated Contact for Authorities

Law enforcement agencies and certified child protection organizations can reach our specialized legal response team at:

Child Safety & Legal Compliance Officer
NEYO Safety Operations
Email: child-safety@neyo.app
Response Time: Priority queue (under 4 hours for child safety referrals)
